4 Madeira bananas (1 1/2 cups approx.)
3L eggs
6 tbsp maple syrup
2 cups rolled oats
1 tsp baking soda
1 tbsp cocoa
1/2 cup chocolate chips or nuts

Loaf pan lined with parchment paper
Oven 180ºC

In a food processor add all ingredients except chocolate chips and blend until smoother.
Transfer the batter to a bowl.  Add the chocolate chips. Put the dough in the loaf pan and bake.
