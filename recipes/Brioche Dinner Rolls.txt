520g flour
1 packet instant yeast
1/2 cup granulated white sugar
1 1/2 tsp salt
1 cup warmed milk 49ºC
2 L eggs + 1 yolk
113g unsalted butter
1 egg for egg wash

Pan 23x33cm lined with parchment paper
Oven 170ºC


In a bowl combine 390g flour with sugar and yeast. Add milk, eggs and salt. Beat with paddle attachment in highspeed for 3 minutes.
Change for the dough hook, add softened butter a little bit at a time, mixing well between each addition. Add 130g flour and knead in highspeed for 10 minutes. The dough will be sticky and should pull away from the sides of the bowl. If not, add an extra flour.
Place the dough in a greased bowl and set aside for 1.30 hour at least. Once the dough has risen, turn it out on a well floured surface and divide in portions of 90g.
Roll each piece in a ball and place in the pan a few cm apart. Cover and let it rise for 1 hour.
Beat the egg and brusg the rolls after the dough has risen.
Bake for 15 to 20 minutes.
