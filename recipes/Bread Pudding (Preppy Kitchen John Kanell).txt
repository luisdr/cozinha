210 to 240g stale bread in little cubes
480ml whole milk
50g granulated white sugar
42g unsalted butter
1 tsp ground cinnamon
1/2 tsp salt
3 L eggs
1 tsp vanilla extract

Square baking dish 20x20cm
Oven 170ºC

In a saucepan combine sugar, milk, butter, cinnamon and salt. Place over medium heat and stir occasionally until butter is fully melted.
Meanwhile butter the dish and place the bread cubes in it.
Once the milk mixture is warm, whisk in eggs one by one and the vanilla extract.
Pour the custard over the bread, making sure to soak any dry pieces. Let soak for 20 to 30 minutes.
Take to the oven and bake for 40-45 minutes or until golden brown. The temperature in the centre should reach 70ªC and the pudding should giggle sligthly.

Vanilla Sauce:
120ml whole milk
120ml heavy cream
50g light brown sugar
28g unsalted butter
2 tbsp cornstarch dissolved in 1/4 cup rum or water
1 tbsp vanilla extract or paste
a pinch of salt
